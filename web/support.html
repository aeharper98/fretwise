Fretwise Support

Help with the Fretwise guitar app.

I can't hear any sound

Turn up your volume and check that "Sound on" is selected (on iPhone, tap the settings button next to the streak badge). Notes play even when the silent switch is on.

Listening doesn't hear my guitar

Fretwise needs microphone permission. On iPhone, open Settings, find Fretwise, and turn on Microphone. Play one string at a time, close to the phone, in a quiet room. The app ignores sound for about a second after it plays a note itself.

The life-size neck doesn't match my guitar

Fretwise draws a 25.5-inch scale, the standard for steel-string acoustics. Shorter-scale guitars (24.75 inches) have slightly narrower frets. If the size looks off, use "Match your screen" on the Life-size tab with a bank card.

Resetting progress

Drill has a "Reset history" button. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all progress.
