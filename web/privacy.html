Fretwise Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

Fretwise does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Microphone

When you turn on the tuner or "Listen to my guitar", Fretwise uses your device's microphone to measure the pitch of the note you play. The sound is analyzed on your device as it happens. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Listening stops when you turn it off or leave that screen.

Your progress

Your settings, practice history and completed lessons are saved only on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) removes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Fretwise project page on GitHub: github.com/aeharper98/fretwise.
