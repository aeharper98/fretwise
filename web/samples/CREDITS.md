# Guitar samples

Acoustic guitar recordings from the [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) collection
(npm package `tonejs-instrument-guitar-acoustic-mp3`), which gathers samples from public-domain sources.
Trimmed to 3.2 seconds with a fade-out and re-encoded as mono 96 kbps MP3. One sample every three semitones;
the app pitch-shifts to fill the gaps.
